## 8. Incident Response Recommendation

### Objective
Establish a clear process for detecting, containing, investigation, and recovering from security incidents.

### Recommendation

1. **Incident Detection and Reporting**
 - Monitor security logs and alerts regularly.
 - Establish a clear process for reporting suspected security incidents.
 - Record the date, time, affected asset, and initial observations.

2. **Containment**
 - Isolate affected systems from the network when necessary.
 - Disable compromised accounts or services.
 - Preserve relevant logs and evidence before making major changes.

3. **Investigation**
 - Review authentication, system, application, and netword logs.
 - Identity the affected systems, accounts, and services.
 - Determine the likely cause and scope of the incident.

4. **Eradication**
 - Remove malicious files, unauthorized accounts, or insecure configurations.
 - Patch affected systems and applications.
 - Reset credentials where compromise is suspected.

5. **Recovery**
 - Restore systems from known-good backups when required.
 - Verify that systems are securely configured before returning them to normal operation.
 - Continue monitoring after recovery for signs of recurring activity.
6. **Post-Incident Review**
 - Document the incident, response actions, and lessons learned.
 - Identify controls that failed or need improvement.
 - Update security procedures and preventive measures based on the findings.

### Priority
**High** - A documented incident response process reduces response time and helps limit the impact of security incidents.
