## Risk Rating Method

Risk is determind using:

**Risk = Likelihood x Impact**

### Likelihood
- Low- Unlikely to occur
- Medium - Possible to occur
- High - Likely to occur

### Impact
- Low - Limited effect
- Medium - Moderate effect
- High - Significant security or business effect

### Priority
- Critical - Immediate action
- High - Prioritixed remediation
- Medium - Remediate in planned cycle
- Low - Address when practical
